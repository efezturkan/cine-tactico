# 06 — Market & Competitive Landscape

**Research snapshot:** 30 October 2026  
**Important:** This is a product/competitor scan, not a paid market-sizing study. Traffic and store figures are snapshots and may change.

## 1. Market conclusion

The idea is not unprecedented. Individual mechanics already exist:
- movie head-to-head ranking;
- daily movie grids and puzzles;
- actor/film connection games;
- user-generated knockout brackets;
- tier lists;
- live audience voting;
- social film profiles.

The opportunity is not “nobody has done this.” The opportunity is to deliver a simple, coherent product that connects solo play, friend competition and creator-generated tournaments with low friction.

## 2. Relevant competitors and benchmarks

### Cineversus
Closest broad product benchmark.
- film/series head-to-head comparisons;
- ELO-style rankings;
- weekly tournaments;
- Taste DNA;
- watchlists and profiles;
- social/live comparison features.

Implication: film comparison and personal taste ranking are not unique differentiators. Do not position the product as a clone with more game modes.

### Callback
Closest multi-mode cinema puzzle benchmark.
- multiple daily movie games;
- grid and connection mechanics;
- actor/film relationships;
- creator-oriented daily themes.

Implication: a collection of movie puzzles already exists. The solo experience must be polished and the social layer must add a clear reason to choose us.

### FavRadar
Important Turkish-language benchmark.
- film trivia;
- live friend rooms;
- quick challenges;
- multiple game mechanics.

Implication: Turkish-language multiplayer is not an empty category. Test its onboarding, room creation, content coverage and retention rather than assuming there is no local competition.

### Cine2Nerdle
- movie connection/grid puzzles;
- battle formats;
- daily challenge mechanics.

Implication: film graph and grid mechanics are established.

### Movie Grid / Framed / Cinemadle / Six Degrees
- daily grid;
- film-frame guessing;
- clues and progressive guessing;
- actor-to-actor connection paths.

Implication: single-player cinema puzzle mechanics are widely explored. Our value must come from execution, content quality and the connected social loop.

### UwUFUFU
- user-created tournament quizzes;
- head-to-head choices;
- creator/community content;
- sharing and discovery.

Implication: tournament creation and shareable brackets are proven product patterns. We should make cinema-specific creation faster and more reliable, not simply reproduce a generic tournament builder.

### BracketFights
- public tournament/bracket creation;
- community voting;
- shareable matchups.

Implication: bracket creation is a known utility. A cinema catalogue, polished mobile voting and game progression can make the experience more focused.

### TierMaker
- user-generated tier lists;
- templates and community ranking.

Implication: tier lists are useful later, but are not necessary to validate the first product loop.

### Letterboxd
- film diary;
- ratings and lists;
- reviews and social discovery.

Implication: do not compete with its core job. Avoid building reviews, diaries and a full film social network.

### TACTICO / Tiki-Taka Toe / Immaculate Grid
- grid-based knowledge competition;
- multiplayer and team variations;
- jokers and additional modes in TACTICO.

Implication: grid games can support repeat play, but the data and answer UX must be excellent. TACTICO user feedback has highlighted friction around text entry, autocorrect, performance, ads and match pacing. Treat this as a UX warning, not as a universal market statistic.

## 3. Competitive positioning

| Product job | Existing market | Our response |
|---|---|---|
| Play a solo cinema puzzle | Callback, Movie Grid, Framed, Cinemadle | Daily Connections with curated, verified content |
| Compare films | Cineversus, FavRadar | Bracket experience connected to solo and social play |
| Compete with a friend | FavRadar, Cine2Nerdle, TACTICO-style mechanics | Simple invite-first Grid Duel, async initially |
| Create a film tournament | UwUFUFU, BracketFights | Catalogue-powered cinema tournament creator |
| Run audience voting | UwUFUFU and creator tools | Later creator room and OBS overlay |
| Track film taste | Cineversus, Letterboxd | Not a launch priority |

## 4. Differentiation hypothesis

Potential wedge:
1. **Solo + multiplayer in one coherent loop.**
2. **Guest-first share links:** people can participate before signing up.
3. **Cinema-native tournament creator:** templates, catalogue search and automatic bracket setup.
4. **Turkish cinema and Turkish series coverage:** carefully curated, not assumed.
5. **Reliable answers:** content is verified and rules are transparent.

This is not yet proven. Validate with a small beta and interviews.

## 5. What not to infer

- Competitor existence does not prove their profitability.
- Website visits are not active users, installs or revenue.
- Store download thresholds are not exact user counts.
- Competitor feature lists do not prove feature usage or retention.
- A gap in a competitor's feature list does not automatically mean user demand.
- Do not claim a market size without a defensible source and method.

## 7. Market validation plan

Before investing in a large build:
- interview 10–15 target users (film fans, friend groups, creators);
- show a clickable prototype of Connections, tournament voting and Grid Duel;
- run a small public tournament campaign;
- measure share-to-join conversion;
- observe whether people finish brackets;
- ask creators to run one test event;
- compare solo return rate with multiplayer invitation rate.

Do not ask only “Would you use this?” Observe actual behavior.
