# 01 — Product Vision & Principles

## 1. Product definition

CINE ARENA is a social competition platform for people who enjoy films and series. Users play short knowledge/connection games, compare titles through tournament brackets, challenge friends and share results.

### Positioning

**“Play cinema. Challenge your taste. Share the winner.”**

The product sits between:
- casual daily puzzle games;
- competitive trivia games;
- film-versus and ranking products;
- user-generated tournament and bracket platforms.

It should feel like a game first, not a database or review community.

## 2. Vision

Make film and series culture playable: turn the titles, people and connections users already know into quick, competitive and shareable experiences.

## 3. Mission

Deliver low-friction games that help users:
- test what they know about cinema;
- discover how their taste compares with friends and the wider community;
- create fun competitions without manually preparing content;
- participate in creator-led and community-led events.

## 4. Product pillars

### PLAY — single-player and multiplayer games
Short sessions, clear rules, immediate feedback and fair scoring.

### VERSUS — comparisons and tournaments
Head-to-head title choices, knockout brackets, community outcomes and shareable winners.

### CREATE — user-generated experiences
Users create a tournament from a catalogue or a simple custom list and share a public link.

### COMMUNITY — lightweight social layer
Profiles, basic stats, public results and creator attribution. This is not a full social network.

## 5. Target users

### Primary: Cinema/series fans
- Enjoy films, series, actors and characters.
- Like quizzes, rankings, debates and “which is better?” content.
- Want a quick game without learning complicated rules.

### Secondary: Friend groups
- Want a low-friction game to play together.
- Share links in WhatsApp, Discord and social media.
- May not want to install an app or create an account.

### Secondary: Streamers and content creators
- Need a quick, visual activity for audience participation.
- Want a bracket/tournament that can be shared or shown on stream.
- Need simple moderation and predictable results.

### Tertiary: Film clubs, student clubs and communities
- Want themed competitions and event activities.
- May need a public link and results page.

## 6. Jobs to be done

1. “I have five minutes. Give me a fun cinema challenge.”
2. “I want to see whether my friend knows more films than I do.”
3. “I want to create a best-of list tournament and share it.”
4. “I want my audience to vote on two films live.”
5. “I want to discover films through other people's choices.”

## 7. Differentiation hypothesis

The product will not win by having the largest number of game modes. It may win by combining:
- a genuinely good solo loop;
- low-friction multiplayer participation;
- user-created cinema tournaments;
- Turkish-language and Turkish-cinema relevance;
- consistent, reliable catalogue data;
- shareable results that bring new players in.

This is a hypothesis to validate, not a proven competitive advantage.

## 8. Product principles

1. **Time-to-fun under 15 seconds:** a returning user should reach a playable action quickly.
2. **One primary action per screen:** avoid dashboard clutter.
3. **Guest-first participation:** public tournament links should open directly.
4. **Short sessions:** solo rounds should usually take 1–5 minutes.
5. **Fairness over cleverness:** no ambiguous answers, hidden scoring or arbitrary matchmaking.
6. **Data quality over catalogue size:** a smaller accurate catalogue is better than a large unreliable one.
7. **One source of truth:** shared catalogue and shared identity across modes.
8. **Progressive complexity:** introduce advanced mechanics only after the core loop works.
9. **No forced social graph:** users can play without following people or adding friends.
10. **No pay-to-win:** monetization must not change competitive outcomes.

## 9. Non-goals for MVP

Do not build:
- a Letterboxd replacement;
- film reviews, long-form comments or discussion forums;
- watch tracking or streaming availability;
- recommendation engine or Taste DNA;
- collectible card packs, loot boxes or card trading;
- complex XP economy, coins or energy systems;
- clans, guilds, leagues and seasons;
- advanced creator analytics;
- native Twitch/YouTube chat integrations;
- AI-generated trivia as an unsupervised source of truth;
- 2v2, battle royale or large-scale matchmaking;
- a full admin CMS beyond the moderation/content tools needed to operate safely.

## 10. Brand and tone

Working name: CINE ARENA. It is not cleared.

Tone:
- energetic, playful, direct;
- cinema-literate but not elitist;
- short copy;
- no corporate jargon;
- Turkish-first, with English-ready localization.

Avoid:
- “the ultimate cinematic universe” style marketing claims;
- fake scarcity;
- aggressive notifications;
- excessive gamification language;
- misleading claims about official partnerships or licensed content.
