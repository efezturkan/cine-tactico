## 1. Overview

Cine-Tactico ("we", "our", or "the App") is an offline-capable 3x3 cinematic strategy puzzle game developed by Efe Öztürkan. We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users.

**In brief: Cine-Tactico does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

When you download, install, and use Cine-Tactico:
- **No User Accounts:** The App does not require registration, login, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, government IDs, device identifiers (IDFA, GAID), or IP addresses.
- **No Location Data:** We do not access, track, or store your GPS or network location.
- **No Device Sensors or Media:** We do not request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, or contacts.
- **No Financial Data:** The current version of the App contains no in-app purchases, payment gateways, or credit card collection.
- **No Tracking or Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook Pixel) or crash tracking services.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage on Your Device

Cine-Tactico operates entirely on your device. Any game preferences you set are stored locally in your device's browser/WebView local storage (`localStorage`):
- **Ambience & Theme Settings (`cinexox_ambience`):** Stores your selected theatre background theme, screen dimness level, film grain preference, and viewfinder colors.
- **In-Memory Game State:** Match progress, bot difficulty, and active scores are maintained in temporary memory during your play session.

**You have full control over this data:** You can clear this data at any time via the in-app "Reset Game Data" button or by clearing the App's storage in your device settings.

---

## 4. Network Connections & Third-Party Services

- **Zero Runtime Data Transmission:** The App makes zero outbound network calls to external servers or APIs during gameplay.
- **Offline Playability:** The App is fully functional without an active internet connection (in Airplane Mode).
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** The current version of the App does not include advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads). No user profile is built for marketing or advertising purposes.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Cine-Tactico is designed for general audiences and film enthusiasts. Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

---

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR & KVKK)

Under applicable data protection laws, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK):
- Because we do not process, collect, or store personal data on any server, we have no personal data to provide, rectify, or delete from a central database.
- You have complete sovereignty over the data stored on your own physical hardware and can delete it at any time.
