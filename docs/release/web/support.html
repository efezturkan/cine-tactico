Does Crosscut require an internet connection?

No. Every mode in Crosscut v1.0 (Classic Duel, Reverse Frame, Cinephile Concept, and Daily Reel) runs 100% offline with no account or sign-in.

Where is my progress and streak stored?

Only on your iPhone. Open Profile → Reset Local Data to clear everything, or uninstall the app to remove all local data permanently.

A film, actor, or director entry looks wrong or missing.

Email us the title and correction using the link above. We review catalog fixes for upcoming updates.

How do I switch between English and Turkish?

Tap the gear icon on the home screen (Settings → Language) to switch between English and Türkçe anytime.
